# OHS Laptop Ergonomics

## Reflection

### What equipment changes can you make to improve your workspace setup?
To improve my workspace setup, I plan to use a laptop stand to raise my screen to eye level, reducing neck strain. Additionally, I will use an external keyboard and mouse to maintain a more natural hand position and prevent wrist discomfort.

### What behavioural changes can you implement to improve posture and reduce strain?
I will ensure that I sit upright with my feet flat on the ground and my back supported. Taking regular breaks to stretch and walk around will also help reduce stiffness and strain from prolonged sitting.

### How can you remind yourself to maintain good posture and take breaks throughout the day?
I will set reminders on my phone to take a break every hour.
