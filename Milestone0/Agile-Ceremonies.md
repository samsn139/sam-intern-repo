## Reflection

### How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment?
Agile ceremonies structure team collaboration and provide regular checkpoints for alignment. They help surface blockers early, clarify goals, foster transparency, and promote accountability.

### Which ceremony is most important for your role, and why?
Daily Stand-ups are the most important for my role. They allow for quick updates, ensure everyone is on track, and help identify issues early, especially when working in a distributed or fast-paced team.
