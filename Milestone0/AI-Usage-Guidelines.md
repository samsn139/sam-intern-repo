# AI Usage Guidelines

## Reflection

### When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?
AI can be useful for:
- Automating repetitive tasks like data formatting or summarization.
- Providing suggestions for code, documentation, or content creation.
- Enhancing productivity by speeding up research and idea generation.

However, I should rely on my own skills when:
- Making critical decisions that require human judgment.
- Ensuring accuracy in sensitive data and information.
- Handling confidential or proprietary company data.

### How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?
- Use AI as a supplementary tool, not a replacement for expertise.
- Always review and validate AI-generated content for accuracy.
- Maintain and develop my own skills to avoid dependency.
- Set clear guidelines on when AI should and should not be used.

### What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?
- Avoid entering sensitive, personal, or confidential information into AI tools.
- Use company-approved AI tools that adhere to security and privacy policies.
- Verify the privacy terms of AI platforms before using them.
- Report any potential security concerns when using AI applications.
