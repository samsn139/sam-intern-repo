# Inclusive Design Reflection

## Reflection

### How can you adjust your communication style to be more inclusive of neurodivergent users and teammates?
- Use clear, concise, and direct language to avoid misunderstandings.
- Be patient and allow extra time for processing information if needed.
- Offer multiple ways to communicate (e.g., written instructions, visual aids, recorded explanations).
- Avoid overwhelming messages—break down complex topics into manageable steps.
- Use structured, predictable formats in communication and documentation.

### What are some common UX or communication pitfalls that might make Focus Bear less accessible or supportive?
- Cluttered or overstimulating UI with excessive animations or distractions.
- Unclear navigation or unexpected changes in interface behavior.
- Overly complex language or jargon that increases cognitive load.
- Lack of customization options for users with different sensory needs.
- Insufficient feedback when users take actions, leading to confusion.

### What is one practical change you can make in your work to better support vulnerable populations?
- Implement more structured and predictable UI elements to reduce cognitive load.
- Ensure text and instructions are straightforward, avoiding unnecessary complexity.
- Allow users to customize settings to match their personal sensory and focus needs.
- Regularly test designs with neurodivergent individuals to get real feedback.
