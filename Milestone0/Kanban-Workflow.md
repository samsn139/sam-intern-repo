## Reflection

### How does Kanban help manage priorities and avoid overload?

Kanban allows teams to focus on a limited number of tasks at a time, making it easier to prioritize what’s most important. It avoids overload by visually highlighting when too much work is in progress, helping the team balance capacity with demand.

### How can you improve your workflow using Kanban principles?

I can improve my workflow by:
- Regularly reviewing the board to reprioritize tasks.
- Moving tasks to the correct columns promptly.
- Limiting the number of tasks I work on at once to maintain focus and quality
