## Reflection

### What is your role, and how does it contribute to the Agile process?

My role is a mobile app developer. I contribute by turning product requirements into functional code, collaborating on technical decisions, and ensuring timely delivery of quality features. I also provide input during planning and retrospectives to improve team performance.

### How do your responsibilities overlap or interact with other roles in the team?

I also work closely with the Product Manager to clarify requirements and align development work with business goals. During retrospectives and daily stand-ups, I share updates and feedback to support transparency and continuous improvement.
