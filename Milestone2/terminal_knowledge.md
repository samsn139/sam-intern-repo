# Terminal Knowledge

## Which terminal client did you choose? Why?
I chose Bash as my terminal client because it is widely used and comes pre-installed on most Unix-like systems such as Linux and macOS. It is simple, highly customizable, and supports a variety of useful commands and scripts for development. 

## What customizations (if any) did you make?
Here are the customizations I made to my Bash terminal setup:
- **Aliases**: I created the following aliases to speed up my workflow:
  alias ll='ls -la'             # List directory contents with detailed information
