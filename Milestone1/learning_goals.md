# Learning Goals

## Reflection

### What do you hope to learn from this internship?
I hope to gain hands-on experience in software development within a startup environment. I want to learn how to apply my technical skills in real-world scenarios and improve my ability to work in a collaborative team setting.

### What skills are you most excited to develop?
I am most excited to develop my proficiency in React Native, and other tools used at Focus Bear. Additionally, I want to improve my debugging, testing, and version control skills.

### What do you think you can contribute to the team?
I believe I can contribute by bringing my educational experience from my bachelor's in software engineering as well as professional experience in data engineering. My ability to quickly learn new technologies and adapt to different environments will allow me to contribute to the team effectively.

### What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?
I feel less confident in resolving merge conflicts, and would certainly like to improve on that.

### What does success in this internship look like for you?
Success in this internship means gaining valuable industry experience, strengthening my technical and soft skills, and making meaningful contributions to the team. I hope to leave with a deeper understanding of software development best practices and the confidence to tackle more complex projects in the future.
