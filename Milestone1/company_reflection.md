# Company Reflection

## 📝 Reflection

### Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
Focus Bear was created to help individuals improve their focus, productivity, and overall well-being. Many people, especially those with ADHD, struggle with maintaining attention and managing distractions. Focus Bear provides structured tools to create better work habits and maintain focus.

### What problem is Focus Bear solving?
Focus Bear addresses the challenge of maintaining focus and productivity, as it helps users establish routines, block distractions, and develop sustainable work habits to enhance their efficiency and overall well-being.

### Why do you think this mission is important?
The mission is important because focus-related struggles can impact people’s education, careers, and personal lives. By providing a structured support system, Focus Bear empowers individuals to manage their attention and tasks effectively, leading to increased confidence and success in their daily activities.

### How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?
I value tools and technologies that support inclusivity and accessibility. Focus Bear aligns with these values by offering solutions that help neurodivergent individuals thrive in work and study environments. Additionally, I appreciate productivity-enhancing tools that encourage mindful work habits and well-being. It is a type of app that I personally would use, as I struggle with focusing on a particular task for a long period of time, and end up getting distracted.

### Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
Yes, I have seen how focus difficulties can impact individuals, whether diagnosed with ADHD or not. Many people, including students and professionals, struggle with distractions and time management. Focus Bear provides structured solutions that can help anyone looking to improve their workflow and maintain better habits.
