# Competitive Landscape

## Reflection

### What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?
Focus Bear is designed specifically for neurodivergent individuals, particularly those with ADHD and Autism, making it unique compared to other productivity apps. It emphasizes habit-building, structured routines, and minimizing distractions in a way that aligns with executive functioning challenges. Unlike general-purpose blockers like Freedom and Cold Turkey, Focus Bear provides a more holistic approach, integrating focus techniques with wellness features.

### If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?
I would choose Focus Bear over competitors because of its tailored approach to neurodivergent users. The app does more than just block distractions—it helps build positive habits, manage transitions between tasks, and reinforce focus-friendly behaviors. Additionally, its customization options allow for flexibility in setting up personalized work routines.

### What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?
Tiimo and Routinery provide more extensive visual scheduling tools, particularly helpful for individuals who rely on visual cues for task management. Focus Bear could benefit from integrating a more detailed timeline view or visual task sequencing feature to enhance its usability for users who thrive with structured, graphical representations of their routines.

### Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?
One improvement Focus Bear could make is enhancing its user interface to be more visually engaging and accessible. Adding a calendar integration or a timeline-based task overview could help users better visualize their daily plans and transitions. This would align with the needs of neurodivergent users who rely on structured, predictable planning tools.
