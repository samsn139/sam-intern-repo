## When do you prefer using AI vs. searching Google?

I prefer using **AI tools like ChatGPT** when:

- I need a clear explanation of a concept in plain language.
- I want help debugging code with a quick response.
- I’m looking for examples or boilerplate code to build upon.
- I want to explore alternative solutions or approaches.

I use **Google** when:

- I’m searching for official documentation or package-specific info.
- I need help from forums like Stack Overflow where others might have faced similar issues.
- I want to compare multiple sources to confirm the accuracy of a solution.
- I’m trying to solve obscure or deeply technical errors that may not be answered by AI.

## How do you decide when to ask a colleague instead?

I ask a **colleague** when:

- The issue is specific to our codebase or infrastructure.
- It’s taking too long to solve on my own.
- I need context or clarification on a team decision or convention.
- The problem is blocking progress and needs immediate resolution.

Before asking, I make sure I’ve:
- Tried debugging myself using AI and Google.
- Collected relevant error messages or logs.
- Narrowed down the problem to save the colleague’s time.

## What challenges do developers face when troubleshooting alone?

- **Overwhelm**: Too much information online can be hard to filter.
- **Tunnel vision**: Sticking to one idea or assumption can block new solutions.
- **Lack of context**: Without understanding the system fully, it's easy to misdiagnose.
- **Wasting time**: Some issues are faster to solve collaboratively.
